POCKETSMART AI – SMART BUDGET & RECOMMENDATION ASSISTANT

PHASE 1: BRAINSTORMING & IDEATION

Problem Statement:
People find it difficult to plan budgets for home interiors, parties, and jewelry purchases without proper guidance. They may spend more than their planned budget or struggle to decide how to allocate their money effectively.

Idea:
PocketSmart AI is an AI-powered smart budget and recommendation assistant. It takes the user's budget and requirements and provides personalized recommendations for Home Interior, Party, and Jewelry planning using the Gemini API.

Target Users:

- Homeowners planning interior decoration
- People planning parties and events
- Individuals looking to buy jewelry within their budget

Empathy Map:

- Users want quick and reliable suggestions.
- Users want to stay within their budget.
- Users want to reduce manual research.
- Users want a simple and easy-to-use web interface.
- Users want personalized recommendations based on their requirements.
